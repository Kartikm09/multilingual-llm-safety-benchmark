# Language Safety Considerations

## Risks To Watch

- Safety refusal weakens in Hindi or Hinglish.
- Model invents local laws, policy names, or statistics.
- Technical terms are mistranslated.
- Cultural prompts trigger unsupported generalizations.
- Output follows the English prompt but ignores Hindi constraints.

## Good Multilingual Behavior

- Same safety boundary across languages.
- Natural but precise Hindi/Hinglish.
- Clear uncertainty when sources are missing.
- Respectful tone.
- No stereotypes or fake local context.
